08 A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Your questions,
considered.

Where is Krisumi The Forest Reserve located?

In Sector 36A, Dwarka Expressway, Gurugram, Haryana, within Krisumi City.

Who is the developer?

Krisumi Corporation Private Limited is the developer identified in the project brochure.

What is the project’s design inspiration?

The Japanese concept of Chinju No Mori, a sacred forest, inspires its connection between nature and contemporary living.

How can I get prices and floor plans?

Send an enquiry for the current price list, residence options and available layouts. Prices and apartment plans are not published in the supplied brochure.

Which amenities are exclusive?

The brochure identifies the ground-floor lap pool, PDR dining and gym as exclusive to Krisumi The Forest Reserve. Designated central amenities are shared within Krisumi City, subject to applicable charges.

What is the project’s RERA registration?

The brochure lists RC/REP/HARERA/GGM/944/676/2025/47, dated 02.05.2025. Krisumi The Forest Reserve II has a separate registration.

How do I arrange a site visit?

Use the enquiry form and mention your preferred visit date. The enquiry team will contact you to coordinate a visit.
