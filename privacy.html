Privacy Policy

This draft describes the enquiry forms and services currently used by this website. The website operator must confirm its identity, contact details and data-handling practices before publication.

Information collected

When you enquire, the form collects the name, mobile number, country calling code, email address and message you provide. The GTF enquiry service also processes visitor identifiers, page addresses, campaign parameters and IP-derived country and city information.

Enquiries and updates

Enquiry information is used to respond to requests about the project, price lists, plans and site visits. The contact-consent wording beside each form describes calls, SMS, email and WhatsApp communications about products and offers. We may send updates to the mobile number or email address registered with us.

Sharing information

Enquiries are processed through GTF. We may share data with Real Estate Regulatory Authority (RERA) registered brokers/companies for further processing as required. The operator must confirm the recipients and arrangements applicable to this website before this draft is approved.

Cookies and external services

The GTF integration uses visitor-identifier cookies and sends page-visit information to its services. The country-code selector uses an IP-based lookup through ipapi.co to suggest a country calling code; you can change the selected country.

Google Fonts and external script providers supply fonts and website libraries. Loading these resources sends normal connection information, including an IP address, to the relevant provider. Browser controls allow you to manage cookies, although restricting external services may affect the enquiry form or country selector.

Privacy enquiries

To raise a question about your information, request a correction or ask about stopping marketing communications, use the website enquiry form and describe your request. This uses the same GTF enquiry service described above.

Draft for review: The operator’s identity, dedicated privacy contact, retention practices and request-handling process remain to be confirmed. This draft is not a legally approved privacy notice.
